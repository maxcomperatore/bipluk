Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about bipluk lifetime access, hardware compatibility, and billing.

Is bipluk really a one-time purchase with no subscription? +

Yes, 100%. bipluk is completely subscription-free. When you buy bipluk+ Lifetime, you pay once ($39 USD or your localized equivalent) and keep access to your cloud soundbank vault forever. There are no recurring monthly or yearly fees, no renewal surprises, and no paywalls on your saved sound designs.

Can I start with the Free plan and upgrade later? +

Yes! You can create a free account with no credit card to test Web MIDI communication with your physical hardware, verify that your browser connects to your MIDI interface, inspect SysEx files in our hex tool, and store 1 soundbank in the cloud. When you are ready for unlimited storage and specialized decoders, upgrading to bipluk+ Lifetime takes less than 30 seconds.

What is the difference between bipluk+ and Studio Commercial? +

bipluk+ Lifetime is designed for individual artists, producers, and collectors who need unlimited patch storage and instant recall across their synths. The Studio Commercial tier is built for commercial sound designers, multi-seat recording facilities, and repair technicians needing commercial soundbank resale rights, multi-user seats, bulk folder migration from legacy software, and custom SysEx decoder scripting.

Which synthesizers are officially supported? +

We offer dedicated patch-name decoders and parameter parsers for the Yamaha DX7 (and DX7II, TX7, TX81Z), Roland Juno-106, Korg M1, Roland Jupiter-6 (Europa-modded), Sequential Prophet-5 / Rev4, Casio CZ series, Oberheim Matrix-1000, and Roland D-50. For any other vintage synth, our Universal ASCII Heuristic Scanner extracts patch names and allows seamless two-way Web MIDI transmission.

Do I need special drivers or software installed on my Mac or PC? +

No background drivers or software installation are needed. bipluk runs directly inside modern web browsers with Web MIDI support (Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge, Opera, Brave). Plug your standard class-compliant USB-MIDI cable into your computer, open bipluk, and your synthesizers are recognized immediately.

How does bipluk prevent buffer overflow errors on older synths? +

Vintage synthesizers from the 1980s utilize slow 8-bit microprocessors that easily crash when blasted with high-speed USB-MIDI data. bipluk automatically paces outbound SysEx dumps into calibrated packages with 60ms pauses, ensuring the receiving instrument can commit the patches to battery-backed SRAM without buffer overflow errors.

Can I access my soundbank vault from multiple computers or tablets? +

Yes! Because bipluk is cloud-native, you can log in from your studio Mac, your stage PC laptop, a Chromebook, or an Android tablet and access all your saved banks instantly without manually copying files via thumb drives.

What payment methods do you accept? +

We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover, Apple Pay, Google Pay, Pix (Brazil), and major regional bank options via Stripe. All payment data is processed securely through Stripe's certified Level 1 PCI DSS infrastructure.
